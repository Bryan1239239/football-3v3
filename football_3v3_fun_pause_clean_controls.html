🎮 HOW TO PLAY
Move: joystick or WASD/arrow keys.
Sprint: hold SPRINT or Shift.
Shoot: drag a line from your player toward the target. Longer line = more power. SHOOT also fires toward goal.
Pass: passes to a teammate and switches to the receiver.
Tackle: clean front/side tackle can win the ball. Tackling from behind can cause a foul.
Set pieces: free kicks, corners, throw-ins and penalties.
Cards: yellow warning, two yellows become red, blue card sends a player off for 60 seconds.
Fun: streaks, crowd reactions, particles, goal celebrations and level progression.
